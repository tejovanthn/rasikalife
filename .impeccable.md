# Rasika.life Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Casual enthusiasts and students of Indian classical music and arts. Primary context: browsing on **mobile while at concerts** — in the moment, looking things up quickly, sharing discoveries with others nearby. Also used at home for deeper research. Skews toward people already embedded in the community (concert-goers, music students) rather than total newcomers.

### Brand Personality
**Vibrant · Community-first · Approachable**

The tone should feel like a knowledgeable friend who loves this music as much as you do — not a cold encyclopedia or a museum archive. Warm, inclusive, enthusiastic without being overwhelming. Celebrating a living tradition, not preserving a relic.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Richer, not minimal** — as the database grows, the interface can breathe and fill with expressive content. Embrace visual density done well.
- **Warm and inviting** — the rust/orange brand color anchors an earthy, culturally resonant palette. Lean into warmth.
- **Mobile-first in reality** — many users are at concerts on phones. Touch targets must be generous, information scannable at a glance.
- **Multi-script native** — Latin, Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, and Kannada all need to render beautifully. Font choice must honor this. Prefer Noto font family (Noto Sans / Noto Serif) for universal script support.
- **Quality bar: production-polished** — not MVP roughness, not obsessive perfectionism. Every screen should feel intentional and complete, but don't agonize over pixel-perfect details.

### Design Principles
1. **Community over catalog** — surfaces that help users connect, contribute, and discover together are more valuable than purely informational displays.
2. **Legible at a glance** — a student checking a raga during a concert has seconds, not minutes. Hierarchy and scanability are paramount.
3. **Culturally resonant** — visual choices should feel native to the context of Indian classical arts: warm earth tones, expressive but not garish, respectful of the tradition's depth.
4. **Mobile-native patterns** — touch targets 44px+, no hover-only interactions on critical paths, content that doesn't require scrolling to identify what something is.
5. **Rich but not overwhelming** — embrace content density as the platform grows, but use whitespace and hierarchy to prevent cognitive overload.
