## Notes

- Lyrics: GSC shows Tamil and Telugu variants already rank 4-7 ("alakalalla lyrics in telugu", "devi padam lyrics in tamil"). Planner shows near-zero volume for generic "in telugu/kannada" terms. Volume is in the song name, not the script. Add script variants per page, not hub pages.
- Biggest lyrics volume is for a few famous songs: bhagyada lakshmi baramma, brochevarevarura, mahaganapathim, endaro mahanubhavulu, krishna nee begane baro, vatapi ganapatim, nagumomu. GSC has zero impressions for bhagyada and vatapi. Competition is high (many lyric sites), so unique content (meaning, raga, composer, notation, multi-script) is the edge.
- Seasonal spikes: Varalakshmi (Aug), Ganesh Chaturthi (Sep), Dec season. Publish and index 4-6 weeks before.
- Event volume is small in Planner but GSC shows steady impressions on venue pages (Bangalore). Venues are the real strength.
- Raga head terms are tiny (melakarta list 260). Individual raga pages already rank 1-3 for long-tail names.
- Quick wins from GSC (position 8-30, impressions >= 20), top by impressions: hamsanandi (3,107), shukra auditorium (1,375), meenakshi rangamancha (1,217), ada rangamandira (1,207), hamsanandi raga (1,134), sri vigna rajam bhaje lyrics (981), dhenuka, engineers club basaveshwaranagar (348), abheri (321). Many are already near position 8-9, so a title and snippet rewrite may reach page 1 top.
- Note: the 500-row export had more rows (has_more=true). The sort was by impressions, so top quick wins are covered.

## Top 5 recommendations

1. Add or fix pages for the 7 famous kritis (bhagyada lakshmi baramma, brochevarevarura, mahaganapathim, endaro mahanubhavulu, krishna nee begane baro, vatapi ganapatim, nagumomu). Total about 60k searches per month. Include lyrics in 2-3 scripts, English meaning, raga, talam, composer. First check if the pages exist and are indexed.
2. Push the position 8-9 pages onto the first page: hamsanandi raga, the three Bangalore venue pages, sri vigna rajam bhaje. Rewrite title tags and meta descriptions, add internal links, add FAQ schema. Cheapest gain.
3. Build city event pages: /events/chennai first (ahead of December season), then Bangalore (improve /events from position 11), Mumbai, Hyderabad, USA. Add a December season 2026 page by sabha.
4. Build raga hubs: melakarta list (72 ragas with arohanam/avarohanam) and janya list, and link each to raga pages. Add "songs in this raga" blocks to raga pages.
5. Add composer hub pages (Purandara Dasa, Tyagaraja, Dikshitar, Syama Sastri) and a varnam hub. Use them as internal link pillars for the lyrics pages. Plan seasonal updates before Varalakshmi and Ganesh Chaturthi 2027.
